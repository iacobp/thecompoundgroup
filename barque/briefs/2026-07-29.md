## Summary

We trimmed one bet today. Barque has a standing prediction that a major US newspaper will publish a big story about people being harmed by experimental drugs called peptides (short protein fragments used for recovery and longevity) before spring 2027. Today we found the raw material for that story has been sitting in public for a year: two women were hospitalised after peptide injections at a Las Vegas anti-ageing festival in July 2025, and Nevada regulators fined the providers in March. No major paper has written the harm story since. They keep writing the "regulators can't keep up" story instead. That is a small reason to be less sure, so we moved from 90% to 87%. Separately, Hims & Hers reports earnings on 10 August — the likeliest single date for it to announce a peptide product of its own.
