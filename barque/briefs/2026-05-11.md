If branded GLP-1 distribution works this smoothly via DTC, the value proposition for compounded GLP-1s weakens structurally — patients can access branded at $25/mo insured or $149/mo cash. Institutional reality distortion at the agency level is a Ring 3 governance signal: if FDA downplays supply disruption in estrogen, how reliably is it characterizing peptide demand or safety data? Not forecasting directly — Ring 3 — but it colors the PCAC deliberation lens.
