## Summary

America's food-and-drug regulator has proposed a new rulebook for pharmacies that mix custom medicines for animals, and is taking public comment until late November. That matters: there is still no approved weight-loss drug for dogs or cats, so anything a vet uses today is custom-made, and this is the rulebook for it.

Hims & Hers, the online health company, told investors this month that it hopes to offer experimental drugs called peptides (short protein fragments used for recovery and longevity) next year — not this one. We had been betting on a launch by December. We lowered that bet today, by the biggest single step since early August.

Medicare's discounted weight-loss drug programme had its biggest month yet in July, the month it opened.

A US appeals court sided with the regulator against pharmacies selling cheap copies of Ozempic.
