## Summary

A Texas pharmacy has recalled injection vials after patients got sick.

Victory Medical Center Pharmacy in Austin pulled three batches of a glutathione shot it makes to order — an antioxidant injection sold by clinics for skin brightening — after testing found bacterial toxin levels high enough to cause fever, dangerous blood-pressure drops or death. Patients had already reported fever, chills, vomiting and racing heartbeats. Vials went to Texas, Florida and New York.

What matters here is the label. The newspaper that broke it called the product "injectable peptides" — experimental drugs, short protein fragments sold for recovery and longevity — and it is not wrong, because glutathione is one. Which means peptides are starting to inherit the safety record of everything sold as an injection in a med-spa. That record is far larger, and far worse.
