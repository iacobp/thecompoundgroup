### Augur picks

**The absent oral presentations — second flag.** Petitioners filed oral presentations to PCAC on June 30. These are the peptide industry's strongest advocacy tool — their best-case safety and efficacy arguments in a format designed for panel persuasion. They have not appeared on the FDA's public docket at T-6, now 12+ business days past filing. The norm is 3–7 days. Three possible reads: (1) administrative backlog at FDA docket management — benign, presentations still reach panelists through internal distribution; (2) FDA staff is reviewing or redacting content before posting — suggests active engagement with the filings; (3) presentations will only surface in the meeting itself — public commenters (deadline July 22) cannot respond to them. If the presentations contain novel human safety data for BPC-157 or KPV, their absence from the public docket limits pre-meeting counter-briefing by opponents like PSM. The Augur notes the anomaly without weighting among explanations.
