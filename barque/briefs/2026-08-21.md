## Summary

Medicare's new discount programme for weight-loss drugs like Ozempic (called GLP-1s) is much smaller than it looks, and we had the size wrong ourselves.

The US Medicare agency lets some older Americans pay $50 a month for these drugs. We had been assuming roughly 14 million people could qualify. The health-policy research group KFF, using government claims data, puts it at **3.8 million**. A further **5.9 million** meet the medical bar but are shut out precisely because they already have a condition the drugs treat — type 2 diabetes, sleep apnoea, or a liver condition. Their alternative runs into hundreds of dollars a month.

So the group locked out is bigger than the group let in. That is the story, and nobody has written it plainly for the people it affects.

Otherwise a quiet day.
