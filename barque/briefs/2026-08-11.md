## Summary

A new menopause drug made headlines yesterday. AbCellera, a biotech, said its injection cut hot flashes sharply, and its shares jumped about a third. The coverage called it a mid-stage trial.

The public trial registry says something smaller. This is a first-in-human study — the earliest kind there is, the first time the drug has been given to people at all. It tests safety first; the hot-flash numbers are a side measurement from a single dose. About 136 volunteers, and it does not finish until February 2027.

That is not a reason to dismiss it. It is a reason not to expect it at a pharmacy soon. Veozah, the last drug of its type to reach patients, needed a full late-stage trial first.

We are writing the honest version. Nothing else moved.
