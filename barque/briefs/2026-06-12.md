# Dawn Brief — 2026-06-12

## Summary

Quiet day. The FDA's advisory panel that will decide whether experimental drugs called peptides (short protein fragments used for recovery and longevity) can be made at specialty pharmacies still has only 3 of 12 seats filled — 27 weeks now without a single new appointment. The oral presentation deadline for that July 23 meeting is 18 days away; if seats remain empty, three career scientists will decide the fate of seven peptides alone.

Meanwhile, Hims & Hers raised its 2026 revenue outlook to $2.8–3.0 billion after shipping 125,000 Wegovy prescriptions in six weeks — solid financial runway for its planned peptide launch. Medicare's $50-per-month weight-loss drug program launches in 19 days; prior authorization paperwork is live. AstraZeneca became the third company to post clinical results for an oral weight-loss pill, deepening a field that keeps getting more competitive.

### Augur picks

**AstraZeneca enters the oral GLP-1 race.** [Elecoglipron Phase 2b results](https://www.healthline.com/health-news/astrazeneca-oral-glp-1-weight-loss-blood-sugar-diabetes) reported at ADA 2026 (published in *The Lancet* June 8): **10.5% weight loss** in T2D patients. Third oral GLP-1 entrant after Novo's Wegovy pill and Lilly's Foundayo. If approved, the oral GLP-1 competitive landscape deepens to 3+ molecules — reducing the consumer case for compounded peptides as alternatives.

**NIH: oral GLP-1s work via brain reward circuits.** [Study](https://www.nih.gov/news-events/news-releases/oral-small-molecule-glp-1-drugs-penetrate-deep-into-brain-suppress-cravings) shows small-molecule oral GLP-1s suppress hedonic feeding through deep brain modulation. A legitimacy upgrade for the class — mechanism moves from "appetite suppression" to "neuroscience of craving control."
