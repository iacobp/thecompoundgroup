# Dawn Brief — 2026-06-11

## Summary

The Department of Justice is stepping up criminal prosecution of sellers dealing in experimental drugs called peptides (short protein fragments used for recovery and longevity). A Utah doctor was indicted in April for selling unapproved peptides to over 200 patients. One of the country's largest specialty compounding pharmacies, Tailor Made Compounding, was forced to forfeit $1.79 million. Two more companies face charges or have pleaded guilty.

This matters because these same peptides — including BPC-157 and TB-500 — are what the FDA's peptide advisory panel will evaluate on July 23. That panel still has nine of twelve seats empty after more than six months. The enforcement climate could give the three remaining career scientists more reason to proceed cautiously.

A prior forecast that The Compound would ship two health properties by summer was confirmed true yesterday.
