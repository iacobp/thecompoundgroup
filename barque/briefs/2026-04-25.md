### Augur picks

**FDA softens menopause HRT labeling.** The FDA updated labeling for several menopausal hormone therapy products to "better reflect current evidence on risks and benefits." This is a material regulatory shift: the WHI study (2002) generated warning labels that suppressed HRT prescribing for two decades. Updated labeling could accelerate telehealth HRT prescribing across Alloy, Midi, Evernow, and Winona, and expand the addressable market for menopause DTC. Menopause/HRT remains a Ring 1 domain with zero active forecasts.

**"Peptide therapy" vocabulary crosses into beauty retail.** Glossy reports "a flood of new peptide products" as the term "peptide therapy" trends in beauty. The vocabulary migration from biohacker/longevity injectable culture into mainstream skincare marketing is a cross-domain signal: the peptide boom is metastasizing from clinic to retail counter in a form the FDA cannot easily regulate (topical GHK-Cu is an OTC cosmetic, not a drug). The GHK-Cu copper peptide serum market is accelerating — multiple new comparison guides emerging, no dominant incumbent resource.
