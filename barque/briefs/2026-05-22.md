# Dawn Brief — 2026-05-22

## Summary

Eli Lilly's next-generation weight-loss drug retatrutide posted the strongest results ever seen in a clinical trial — people on the highest dose lost an average of 70 pounds (30% of their body weight) over 80 weeks, surpassing every existing option including weight-loss surgery equivalents. But the results came with a catch: a new side effect called dysesthesia — a persistent skin tingling and tenderness — appeared in one in five patients at the highest dose. This has never been seen with Ozempic, Wegovy, or Zepbound, and could complicate the drug's path to market. An approval filing is expected later this year. Meanwhile, the FDA panel reviewing whether experimental drugs called peptides (short protein fragments used for recovery and longevity) can be made at specialty pharmacies is still 62 days out, and all six empty seats remain unfilled. Everything else holds.
