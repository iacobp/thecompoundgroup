### Augur picks

**The 2024 vote pattern as floor estimator.** Across 2024 PCAC hearings, 13 peptide substances received approximately 11 Yes votes against 167 No. Even Thymosin Alpha-1 — which had the strongest international clinical record — lost 4-17-0. This is the institutional muscle memory the reconstituted panel inherits. The Augur notes that while the panel roster changed, the FDA staff who write the briefing documents and frame the questions did not. The format of the hearing — staff presentation first, then public comment, then panel vote — gives staff significant framing power regardless of who sits in the chairs. If the new panel nonetheless overrides staff on ≥2 of 4 peptides, it would be the most consequential departure from staff recommendation in PCAC history. Worth tracking as a calibration event for future advisory-committee forecasts across all domains.
