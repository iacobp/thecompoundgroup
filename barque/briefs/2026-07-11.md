### Augur picks

**FDA docket posting delay for PCAC oral presentations.** The June 30 oral presentation filings have not appeared on the FDA docket as of July 11 — 8 business days past deadline. Typical posting lag is 3–7 business days. Two explanations: (a) administrative backlog from heavy submission volume on a controversial panel, or (b) FDA staff review before posting. If (b), it would be unusual but not unprecedented; staff review of oral presentations could indicate redaction of unsubstantiated claims or annotation of commercially biased content. Either way, the presentations will be posted before the July 23 meeting. Worth monitoring next week — a late posting with extensive redaction would shift the informational landscape panelists enter the room with.
