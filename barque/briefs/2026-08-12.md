## Summary

Two quiet, useful findings today.

A large British study followed 182,000 women with asthma in their late forties and fifties. Some started hormone replacement therapy; most did not. The women taking hormones had no more asthma attacks than the women who were not. That is a clear no — and the coverage around it is written as though the answer were murky.

Separately, an older weight-loss drug called liraglutide has been on the US drug regulator's shortage list without a break since July 2023. The regulator re-confirmed that last week. The branded version, Victoza, is still hard to get; the cheaper generic is not. Almost nobody writes about this drug now, because attention moved on to Ozempic and the newer injections.

We are writing both up.
