### Augur picks

**ACHIEVE-3 tolerability gap — a quiet disconfirming signal for Foundayo dominance.** The Lancet head-to-head published February 26 and being presented at ADA confirms Foundayo's weight-loss superiority (73.6% greater relative reduction at 52 weeks). But the discontinuation data tells a different story: [roughly twice as many patients quit Foundayo](https://www.hcplive.com/view/achieve-3-orforglipron-superior-to-semaglutide-for-type-2-diabetes-in-phase-3-trial) (8.7–9.7% vs 4.5–4.9% for semaglutide). This doesn't move any forecast today, but it matters for two downstream dynamics: (1) physician prescribing patterns — a 2x discontinuation gap can shift formulary preference toward the tolerable option, especially in Medicare where prior auth creates switching friction; (2) the DTC advertising narrative — "more weight loss but harder to stay on" is a complex story for a Hims/Found/Ro comparison page. Watch whether the tolerability gap narrows in longer-duration extensions or expands.

**Estrogen patch shortage: 184% prescription increase, 44% difficulty filling, multi-year duration.** [Midi Health survey](https://www.joinmidi.com/post/estrogen-patch-shortage-data) of 8,000 women found 44% report difficulty filling estrogen patch prescriptions. HRT prescriptions for women 45–54 up 184% since 2018. [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/health/womens-health/fda-estrogen-patch-shortage-hormone-therapy-alternatives-rcna344233) reports FDA disputes the shortage while patients confirm widespread stockouts. Shortage expected to last 3+ years with only two manufacturers supplying the US market. This doesn't touch a pending forecast but strongly reinforces the HRT Picks thesis and the ongoing `hrtpicks-update-estrogen-patch-fda-denial` work order.
