# Dawn Brief — 2026-06-08

## Summary

A new player entered the peptide telehealth market this week. OneTwenty, a DTC wellness platform, launched in June with a $499 membership that bundles lab panels, wearable data, AI coaching, and telehealth visits — plus access to compounded peptides through a pharmacy network. They are positioning to carry peptides "the moment the FDA's July review clears them." It is the first company besides Hims & Hers to build commercial infrastructure at scale around the assumption that the FDA's peptide advisory panel will vote favorably on July 23.

Meanwhile, Novo Nordisk announced that its weight-loss pill (called Wegovy tablets) has crossed three million prescriptions in just over five months — making it one of the fastest pharmaceutical launches in US history by volume. One prescription is filled roughly every five seconds. Separately, Stanford researchers found that an existing drug, already in clinical trials for age-related muscle wasting, can prevent muscle loss caused by weight-loss drugs like Ozempic in mice — addressing one of the most common complaints from patients on these medications.

The government's $50-per-month Medicare weight-loss drug program launches in 23 days, but the paperwork doctors need to enrol patients has still not been released. None of Barque's forecast probabilities moved this week. Quiet day for calibration.
