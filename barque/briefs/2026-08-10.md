## Summary

The useful thing today was one of our own mistakes, found and fixed.

Last month a panel advising the US drug regulator backed six of seven experimental drugs called peptides — short protein fragments sold for recovery and longevity. We have been watching one government noticeboard for the follow-through, and treating its silence as bad news for a forecast. A national radio feature we had missed, sitting in our own files since 3 August, quotes a lawyer for the pharmacies involved saying he would be surprised if that noticeboard were used at all. We were measuring the right thing in the wrong place. The forecast went back up.

The same report says that even after the rules change, pharmacies need two to nine months to get supplies. That pushes a lot of things into next year.

Quiet otherwise.
