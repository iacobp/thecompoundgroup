## In plain English

The US Medicare agency quietly delayed part of a program meant to expand access to weight-loss drugs like Ozempic (called GLP-1s) for people on government insurance. The delay, reported this week, hints that the plumbing behind Medicare's $50-per-month GLP-1 program for seniors — set to launch July 1 — may be harder to build than the policy itself. That makes it more likely the program starts slowly, which is what Barque already expects.

Separately, Hims & Hers confirmed that the peptide manufacturing facility it bought last February is now operating and doing active research. That removes one obstacle to launching experimental drugs called peptides (short protein fragments used for recovery and longevity) through its telehealth platform this year.

Quiet day otherwise. The FDA's peptide advisory panel is 88 days away. No new safety incidents reported.
