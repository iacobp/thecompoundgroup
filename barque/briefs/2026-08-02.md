## Summary

A company you have never heard of quietly did what everyone expects the big one to do. On 7 July — sixteen days before a US advisory panel backed six experimental drugs called peptides (short protein fragments used for recovery and longevity) — a firm called Precision Peptide began selling them online in all fifty states, with a US pharmacy agreement and the certification a health seller needs to advertise. So the question is no longer whether a legitimate-looking way to sell these can exist before the rules do. One is already running.

Ten days after the vote, the federal rulebook still shows nothing filed.

Medicare's new weight-loss drug programme is thirty-three days old with no enrolment figure published, and we confirmed at the source that it was built to run outside the very records our prediction says to check.
