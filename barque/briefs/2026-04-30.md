## In plain English

The biggest thing Barque missed this month: back in November, the US government quietly removed its strongest safety warnings from hormone replacement therapy — the medications millions of women use for menopause symptoms. Since then, prescriptions have roughly doubled. Demand for estrogen patches has surged 184% since 2023. The country is now running short, with one major patch brand in nationwide shortage and supply potentially tight for years.

This pattern looks familiar. Two years ago, a regulatory shift on weight-loss drugs like Ozempic unlocked pent-up demand, overwhelmed supply, and opened a secondary market at specialty pharmacies. The same template is now playing out in hormone therapy.

None of our seven tracked forecasts moved today. Hims & Hers (the telehealth company) reports earnings in 11 days.
