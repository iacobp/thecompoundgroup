## In plain English

A week of confirmation rather than surprise. Hims & Hers confirmed that the peptide manufacturing facility it bought in California last February is now operating and doing active research — its stock jumped 49% in five days. A launch of recovery and longevity products through its telehealth platform this year looks increasingly likely, though an upcoming earnings report on May 11 will test whether the rally is built on revenue or hope.

The FDA's peptide advisory panel meets in 87 days with six empty seats, including the chair. In pet health, two separate cat weight-loss drug trials are underway, and a dog longevity pill cleared another FDA safety hurdle.
