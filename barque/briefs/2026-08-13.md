## Summary

Hims & Hers (a large online health company) told investors this week that it plans to sell experimental drugs called peptides — short protein fragments used for recovery and longevity. Several news outlets reported it. We went and checked the company's actual filings with financial regulators, and found nothing at all. So this is a stated plan, not a done deal, and we are writing it up that way rather than the way the headlines did.

Separately, a US university has registered the first proper trial of whether a muscle supplement protects people losing weight on drugs like Ozempic (called GLP-1s). The trial has not started yet. It reports in 2030. Shops are already selling that answer today.

And our own search data threw up something odd: our best page is seen far more than it is clicked.
