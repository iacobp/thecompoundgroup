## Summary

Yesterday we reported that headlines said 600,000 people had signed up for a US government scheme selling weight-loss drugs like Ozempic (called GLP-1s) to older Americans for $50 a month — and that we could not find where the number came from. Today we found it. Reuters filed it on 31 August, quoting the head of the US Medicare agency.

That changes our view. A month earlier the same official said 250,000. Two months in, 600,000. The scheme is speeding up, not slowing down. Our forecast said it would stay under a million people by the end of December, and we have cut our confidence in that from 55% to 35%.

One caution stays. "Signed up" is not the same as "approved and enrolled", and the agency itself has still published no figure of its own.

## Signal detail

**`medicare-glp1-bridge-uptake`: 0.55 → 0.35. Flagged.**

*Historian.* The reference class said year-one participation in a friction-laden Medicare programme runs 10–15% of eligible. Two months in, this one is at roughly 15.8% of the 3.8M pool KFF verified. The base rate is already cleared at month two, so the reference class is not governing this case and I should say so rather than retrofit it. *Skeptic.* The counter-case is unchanged and not weak: every figure in the series is a sign-up claim by a political appointee, not a data release. Reuters reports faithfully what Oz *said*. The row resolves on enrolees per official Medicare prescription-programme data, and that source structurally cannot publish this quantity because the scheme was built outside it. A sign-up curve clearing a million does not make this forecast false; it may make it unresolvable.

*Bayesian.* Prior 0.55. The new evidence is not the 600,000 alone — it is that the series now has two points from one speaker: 250,000 at day 29, 600,000 at day 61. Month two added 350,000 against month one's 250,000. To stay under a million, the remaining four months must average 100,000, a ~70% collapse in pace starting now. Nothing predicts one. Posterior **0.28**.

Its newsroom answered 200 again today and carries no Bridge enrolment release — the front page is lab-fraud enforcement and four rural telehealth grants. An administration happy to announce 600,000 on video and unwilling to publish it as data is saying something about how the number was built.
